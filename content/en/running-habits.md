## Start with a week you can repeat

NHS Couch to 5K starts with three runs a week and rest days between, then builds over nine weeks or longer from one-minute runs with 1.5-minute walks toward 30 minutes of continuous running. The value is not the number “nine”: it is a fixed frequency, planned rest and a small increase at a time.

## Make the goal executable

Turn “I should start running” into a weekly plan: which three days, how long, where, and what happens when it rains or work runs late. The ODPHP activity planner can help choose preferred activities, set goals and print a weekly tracking plan. A plan that works on an ordinary weekday is more reliable than one that only works under ideal conditions.

## Consistency beats one hard session

The CDC recommends at least 150 minutes of moderate-intensity activity a week and muscle-strengthening activity on two days. Activity can be spread through the week and broken into smaller bouts. For a beginner, “three easy sessions I can complete each week” is more useful than one very long or very fast session. Easy running, run-walk and walking all count as activity.

## A missed session is not failure

When a plan is interrupted, return at a similar but slightly lower load rather than cramming all the missed work into one “make-up” session. If fatigue, sleep or pain persists for several days, reduce running and observe. If pain continues or affects walking, see [pain and load](/en/articles/pain-and-load/) and seek professional assessment when needed.

## Track trends, not perfection

Record date, duration, how it felt, sleep and pain, not only pace. Every two or three weeks, review what conditions made sessions most likely to happen and which arrangements were always skipped. Keep the successful conditions and shrink unrealistic goals. Habits are built by making the right choice easier, not by relying on willpower.

## What this page does not provide

This page does not give an individual training plan, promise weight loss or performance, or replace medical advice about exercise. People with cardiovascular, metabolic, bone/joint or other health conditions should consult a qualified clinician before starting. Stop and seek emergency help for chest pain, fainting or unusual breathlessness.

## Sources and scope

The three-runs-a-week progression follows NHS Couch to 5K; adult activity targets follow the CDC; goal setting and weekly tracking follow the ODPHP activity planner. These are general public-health and habit suggestions, not an individual training prescription.
